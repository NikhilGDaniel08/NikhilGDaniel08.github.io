Nikhil Daniel

Harvard College, Class of 2030

I’m a first-year at Harvard studying Social Studies and Statistics. I’m interested in how societies govern powerful technologies, international markets, and geopolitical risk.

Right now, I do AI and policy research with Orit Shaer, a fellow at the Harvard Radcliffe Institute. I’m also an AI Policy Fellow with Harvard’s AI Safety Student Team.

Outside of that, I co-founded Clusion, a prep and rankings platform for competitive speech and debate. I also compete with the Harvard College Debating Union, am an associate for Ambassador Chris Lu at the Institute of Politics, and serve on the advisory board of Outreach Debate, a nonprofit that makes speech and debate accessible to students around the world, where I was previously an executive.

Before Harvard, I spent most of my time on journalism, debate, housing policy, and geopolitics. I was twice named Florida Journalist of the Year (Online and Emerging), was ranked third in the country in Public Forum debate, won the Congressional App Challenge for Rep. Maxwell Frost’s district with software that helps homeless service providers manage their work, and had work published through the UN Human Rights Committee. I was also deeply involved in housing research and policy, and worked on technology innovation at the Homeless Services Network of Central Florida.

Research

AI, policy, and the people who live with both.

  • 2026–

    AI and policy research, Harvard Radcliffe Institute

    With Orit Shaer, the 2026–27 Sally Starling Seaver Fellow at Radcliffe and a professor of computer science at Wellesley College, where she directs the Human-Computer Interaction Lab. Her Radcliffe project is a book, Weaving Wonder: Human Flourishing in the Age of Artificial Intelligence, which proposes a framework for designing and evaluating AI systems, drawing on cases from design, education, and emergency medicine.

  • Fall 2026

    AI Policy Fellow, AI Safety Student Team

    A small-group seminar on the pace of AI progress, risks from misuse and misalignment, and the policy responses on the table: model audits and evaluations, liability, transparency, compute and semiconductor governance, and international coordination.

  • High school

    Technology innovation and housing policy, Homeless Services Network of Central Florida

    HSN is the lead agency for Central Florida’s response to homelessness across Orange, Osceola, and Seminole counties. It funds shelter and housing programs, runs the region’s coordinated-entry system, and tracks homelessness data to inform policy. I worked on technology innovations there alongside research on housing policy.

  • High school

    Winner, Congressional App Challenge, Florida’s 10th District

    Selected by Rep. Maxwell Frost as the winning app for his district in the House of Representatives’ nationwide coding competition for middle and high school students. The app helps homeless service providers manage their work.
