Work

Research, policy, and public service.

  • 2026–

    AI and policy research, Harvard Radcliffe Institute

    With Orit Shaer, the 2026–27 Sally Starling Seaver Fellow at Radcliffe and a professor of computer science at Wellesley College, where she directs the Human-Computer Interaction Lab. Her Radcliffe project is a book, Weaving Wonder: Human Flourishing in the Age of Artificial Intelligence, which proposes a framework for designing and evaluating AI systems, drawing on cases from design, education, and emergency medicine.

  • 2026–

    Associate to Ambassador Chris Lu, Institute of Politics

    Ambassador Lu is a resident fellow at the Harvard Kennedy School Institute of Politics. He previously served as U.S. Ambassador to the UN for Management and Reform, Deputy Secretary of Labor, and White House Cabinet Secretary under President Obama.

  • Fall 2026

    AI Policy Fellow, AI Safety Student Team

    A small-group seminar on the pace of AI progress, risks from misuse and misalignment, and the policy responses on the table: model audits and evaluations, liability, transparency, compute and semiconductor governance, and international coordination.

  • 2025–26

    System Improvement and Tech Innovations Intern, Homeless Services Network of Central Florida

    HSN is the lead agency for Central Florida’s response to homelessness across Orange, Osceola, and Seminole counties. It funds shelter and housing programs, runs the region’s coordinated-entry system, and tracks homelessness data to inform policy. I worked on system improvements and technology innovation alongside research on housing policy.

  • Summer 2024

    Legislative Intern, Florida House of Representatives

    Interned for Representative Anna V. Eskamani of District 42.

Publications

Reports submitted to the United Nations Human Rights Committee.

  • March 2025

    NGO Report on Burkina Faso for the 143rd Session of the Human Rights Committee ↗

    Co-authored on behalf of CVIA and presented at the UN Office at Geneva. The report documents political violence and the suspension of elections under military rule, gaps in civic education, and barriers to women’s participation in political life.

  • October 2024

    NGO Report on Pakistan for the 142nd Session of the Human Rights Committee ↗

    Co-authored on behalf of CVIA and presented at the UN Office at Geneva. The report covers unequal political representation for women and girls, weak civic education, political violence, limits on free expression, and corruption.
